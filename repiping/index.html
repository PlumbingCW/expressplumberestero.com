Repiping in Estero, FL

Repiping is a planned project, not a rush job. Call for an assessment, and we will tell you whether a repair or a repipe fits your house.

Call (239) 522-0887

Signs the pipes are wearing out.

Callers rarely ask for a repipe first. They describe a stain on the ceiling, a bill that keeps climbing, or a second leak in the same year. When leaks keep returning in different spots, the pipe itself is failing, not one fitting.

Some callers ask about repiping both supply lines and drain lines at once. The two are separate systems and are often done in separate phases. We look at what is actually failing before quoting either.

Signs worth a repipe conversation.

  • Repeat leaks in different parts of the house, especially pinhole leaks.
  • Brown or rusty water when you run a tap after it has sat.
  • Pressure that drops across the house, not at one fixture.
  • Visible green, white or flaking corrosion on exposed pipe.
  • A meter that keeps moving with all fixtures off.

The age of the house and what to ask.

Estero homes and pipe age.

Many Estero homes were built between the late 1990s and the mid 2000s. Shadow Wood, for example, was built from 1998 to 2005, which puts many houses at roughly 20 to 35 years old. We do not assume a pipe material by community, so a plumber has to look.

That age is when you decide between repair and a planned repipe. If you are buying or selling in this range, have a plumber inspect before you commit.

Slab houses and access.

Most Estero houses sit on a slab, so some pipes run in the slab or in the attic. Reaching them can mean opening walls or ceilings. A slab leak may be fixed with a reroute through the attic instead of breaking floors. See slab leak repair for that path.

We cannot tell from the phone which route fits your house. A site visit and sometimes a leak locate decide it.
