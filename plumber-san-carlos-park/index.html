A neighborhood older than its neighbors.

San Carlos Park was founded in 1953 by brothers Jules and Jack Freeman, who bought nearly 250 acres from the Koreshan Unity. They wanted low-cost homes. That makes much of it older than the gated communities in Estero. Older houses mean older plumbing, and that shapes most of our calls here.

We cannot tell a pipe material from the phone. A plumber has to look. What we can say is that homes from several decades back deserve a close check of shutoff valves, supply lines and the heater.

What to check in an older home.

Shutoff valves.

Turn each one gently. A valve that will not close is worth replacing before an emergency.

Supply lines.

Look for braided or flexible lines that are bulging, rusted or crusted white.

Water heater age.

Find the date on the label. A unit well past a decade can fail without warning.

Main valve.

Know where it is and make sure everyone at home can reach it.

Creeks, rain and drains.

Mullock Creek and low spots.

Mullock Creek drains west toward Estero Bay, and US-41 forms the community's western edge. After heavy rain, ask whether standing water in the yard is runoff or a leak. A lawn that stays soggy in dry weather points to a line problem.

A running meter with every tap off settles it. If the dial moves, call us for leak detection before the ground stays wet for weeks.

Unincorporated means Lee County.

San Carlos Park is not a city or village. Permit questions go through Lee County, so ask Lee County whether your water heater swap, repipe or sewer repair needs one. Rules vary and we will not guess for you.

For water, check your bill for the utility name. Lee County Utilities serves north of Williams Road in the Estero area, but we will not assume your provider. The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, 8th Edition took effect on December 31, 2023, and newer permits follow it.

Drains that keep coming back.

Many callers tell us a drain was cleared once and slowed again within weeks. That pattern often means buildup further down or a line problem a plunger cannot reach. Never mix drain chemicals, and avoid pouring them into a line that is already standing full.

A camera look decides whether you need drain cleaning, hydro jetting or a repair. We cannot know which from the phone.
