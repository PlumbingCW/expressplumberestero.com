How to read this list.

Estero Express Plumbing is a licensed and insured plumbing team serving the Village of Estero and nearby towns. The cards below cover every service we offer, from clogged drains to whole-house repiping. Pick the one that matches your symptom, or call and describe the problem in plain words.

Most callers start with a symptom, not a service name. A wet ceiling, a bill that keeps climbing, a toilet that will not stop running. You do not need to know the trade term. Dispatch will match what you describe to the right visit.

Emergency plumbing that cannot wait.

Some problems get worse by the hour. Water moving through a wall, sewage coming up in a tub, or a tank leaking onto the floor all fall in this group. Our line is open 24/7 and a person answers.

  • Pipes that have burst or are spraying: burst pipe repair.
  • Water you cannot account for, such as a hot spot on the floor or a meter that moves with everything off: slab leak repair and leak detection.
  • Main line backups and sewage in tubs or floor drains: sewer line cleaning and sewer line repair.
  • A heater that is leaking or has gone cold: water heater repair or water heater replacement.
  • Drains that stop completely: drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

Do this before you call.

Shut off the water at the main valve if water is moving where it should not. Turn off power to the area if water is near outlets or appliances.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside. Do not flip switches on your way out.

Everyday repairs inside the house.

Smaller fixtures often feel like they can wait. A running toilet or a dripping tap wastes water every hour, though, and small leaks tend to grow. These repairs are usually quick once a plumber is on site.

Fixture and appliance repairs.

  • Toilet repair for running, weak flushing, loose bases or slow refills.
  • Faucet repair for drips, low flow or handles that no longer seal.
  • Garbage disposal repair for humming, jams or leaks under the sink.
  • Drain cleaning for sinks, tubs and showers that drain slowly.

Planned work you can schedule.

Not every job is urgent. If you are updating a bathroom, buying a house or planning ahead, scheduled work lets you compare options before anything is opened up. We offer same-day appointments when available, and planned visits are easier to fit in.

Planned work includes water heater installation, tankless water heater service, repiping, plumbing installation and water softener and filtration. A sewer camera inspection belongs here too, because it shows what is inside the line before you decide on repairs.

Homes and businesses.

Many Estero homes sit in gated communities built from the late 1990s through the mid 2000s, so plumbing that is now 20 to 35 years old is common. That is a reason to pay attention to small warning signs, not a prediction about any one house.

We also take commercial plumbing calls for shops, offices and rental properties. Landlords and property managers can call on behalf of tenants. Tell dispatch who will be on site to let us in.

How pricing works.

What shapes the cost of any plumbing job.

Scope of the problem.
A single fixture is quicker than a line that runs through several rooms.
Access.
Open crawl spaces and garages are easier than walls, ceilings and slabs.
Parts and materials.
Pipe type, fixture grade and equipment size all change the total.
Time of day.
Urgent after-hours calls take priority scheduling, and that can affect the quote.
Code and permits.
Some jobs need permits. Rules vary, so ask the Village of Estero Community Development department.

You get a clear price before work starts. If the job changes once a wall is open, we stop and explain first.
