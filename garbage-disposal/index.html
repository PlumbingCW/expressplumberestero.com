Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Estero, FL

A disposal that hums but will not spin, or a sink that fills with gray water, is one of the most common kitchen calls. Call and we will find the cause.

Call (239) 522-0887

What the sound and the symptom tell us.

A disposal gives clear clues. A hum with no spinning means the blades are jammed. Silence means a tripped overload, a dead outlet or a failed motor. Water under the sink points to a seal or a drain fitting.

Callers often describe a sink that will not drain with the disposal running. That can be the disposal, or it can be the line beyond it. We check both before we quote anything.

Safe steps before we arrive.

What to do and what to leave alone.

  • Turn the disposal switch off, then unplug it or flip its breaker. Never reach in by hand.
  • Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit once it has cooled.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a sink with standing water. It can splash and burn skin.
  • Stop running water into the sink if it is not draining.
  • Move cleaning supplies out of the cabinet so the plumber has room.

Repair or replace the disposal.

A jam or a tripped reset is quick to fix and the unit keeps running. A cracked housing or a seized motor is not repairable. At that point a new unit costs less than repeated visits.

If you are replacing, tell us the horsepower of the old unit and whether you want it quieter or stronger. A household that cooks heavily gets more from a larger motor. We also check the dishwasher drain connection, since it joins the disposal.

What not to put down the disposal.

  • Grease, oil and fat, which cool and coat the drain line.
  • Fibrous food such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.
  • Coffee grounds in bulk, bones, pits and fruit stones.
  • Pasta, rice and potato peel, which swell into a paste.
  • Eggshells in large amounts, or any non-food item.

When the disposal is only the symptom.

If water backs up into the second sink basin, or other drains gurgle, the clog is deeper than the disposal. See drain cleaning for a kitchen line, or hydro jetting when grease has built up in the pipe.
