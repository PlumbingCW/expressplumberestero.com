Where Grandezza is and what it includes.

Grandezza is a gated community in Estero with ZIP 33928. A Homes.com guide places it between Ben Hill Griffin Parkway and Corkscrew Road, and a 2013 planning study describes it east of I-75, south of Florida Gulf Coast University and Miromar Lakes.

Sources count about 950 to 1,000 homes across roughly a dozen neighborhoods. Names include Avalon, Cypress Cove, Grande Estates, Sabal Palm, Santa Lucia, Saraceno, Savona, Solemar and Villa Grande. Homes range from condos, villas and coach homes to single-family houses and custom estates.

A real estate guide lists a median build year of 2005, which makes the average Grandezza home about 21 years old. We do not state the age of any one building. Sources are listed in the note further down.

Seasonal homes and slow leaks.

The same guide calls Grandezza a popular vacation home choice among retirees. Not every owner lives here all year, and we cannot say how many do not. For any home left empty for weeks, a small leak has time to turn into a large one.

Water pressure stays on in the pipes while nobody is home. A weeping supply line, a failed toilet valve or a heater that starts to leak will keep running until someone notices.

Before you leave for the season.

Shut off the main.

Closing the main valve means a failed fixture cannot flood the home while you are away. Check with your insurer first, since some policies ask for it.

Check the water heater.

Look at the tank base and the relief line. Ask us whether to turn the heater down or off while you travel.

Run every fixture.

Flush toilets and run taps to look for slow drains before you go.

Leave a contact.

Give a neighbor or manager a key and tell them to call us about any water.

Coming back to a wet floor or a high water bill? Call us right away.

Coming home to a closed-up house.

  1. 1Open the main slowly.Listen for hissing or running water inside the walls.
  2. 2Read the meter.Note the dial, wait a few minutes with every tap off and read it again.
  3. 3Run hot and cold taps.Let water run until it clears. Stale water in lines can look discolored at first.
  4. 4Look under sinks.Check supply lines, shutoff valves and the garbage disposal base.
  5. 5Call if the dial moves.A moving meter with every tap off means water is escaping. Book leak detection to find it.

A disposal that sat idle can also jam. Our garbage disposal page explains when to try a reset and when to call. Stuck drains after an empty season are common, and drain cleaning clears the line instead of the symptom.

Condos, villas and houses use different lines.

Avalon and Sabal Palm have condos, villas and coach homes according to the Homes.com guide, while other neighborhoods hold larger single-family houses. In a shared building, some pipes may belong to the association, so report a leak to your manager in writing.

In a freestanding house the whole system is yours, from the main shutoff to the last fixture. Tell dispatch which situation you are in.
