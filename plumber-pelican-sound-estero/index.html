A gated community of many small neighborhoods.

Pelican Sound is a gated golf and river community at the west end of Corkscrew Road, near US-41. A 2013 planning study counted more than 1,000 homes on about 500 acres. A real estate profile describes about a dozen subdivisions with estate homes, executive homes, coach homes, carriage homes and condominiums.

Named neighborhoods in that study include Edgewater, Island Sound, Oak Run, Palmetto Dunes, Pinehurst, Seaside, Southern Hills and Turnberry. We do not publish a boundary for each one, because we could not find an official map.

Sources disagree on when the community was built, and one profile gives 2003 as a development year. Because we could not confirm it, we do not state the age of any Pelican Sound building. Check your own deed or ask your association.

When your plumbing is part of a building.

In a condo or coach home, the water supply, drain stack and sometimes the water heater may serve more than one unit. A slow drain in your first-floor kitchen can come from a clog shared with the unit next door.

Governing documents decide what the owner repairs and what the association repairs. Read them, or call the manager, before any pipe inside a wall is cut open.

Leaks that travel.

Water from a failed supply line or tank can run along a floor slab or through a ceiling and show up far from the cause. Stained drywall, a musty corner or a floor that feels warm underfoot all point to hidden water.

A running meter with every tap off settles the question for your unit. For a hidden line we use leak detection to trace the source before walls are opened. A shut-off that fails to hold calls for burst pipe repair.

What we do not claim about your street.

We do not state a flood risk, soil condition or pipe material for any Pelican Sound street, because we found no public source for them. Ask your association or insurer about your lot, and ask a plumber to look at your own lines.

Gutters, drain lines and yard drains only handle the water they are built for. If a yard drain stands full for days after rain, call us for a drain cleaning look.

Sources: Estero Community Market Assessment (2013), Naples Condo Boutique profile of Pelican Sound.

Permits and utility questions.

Permits depend on the job. The Village of Estero's Community Development department can tell you whether yours needs one, and your association may want to approve the work as well.

Your water bill names your utility. The Village splits service at Williams Road, but we do not assume where your building falls, so read the bill.
