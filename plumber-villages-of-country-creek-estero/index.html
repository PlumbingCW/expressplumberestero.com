Plumber in the Villages of Country Creek, Estero, FL

The Villages of Country Creek lies north of Corkscrew Road in Estero, with villas, manor homes and houses around a public executive golf course. Some of its homes are older than the gated communities nearer I-75.

Where Country Creek sits.

The Villages of Country Creek is on the north side of Corkscrew Road in Estero, about half a mile west of Three Oaks Parkway according to a 2013 planning study. That study counted 985 units on 235 acres. A real estate profile says 966 homes were built by Weiner Homes, so we say roughly 970 to 985.

Homes include single-family houses, attached villas, manor homes and condominiums. An 18-hole executive golf course designed by Gordon Lewis is open to the public. The profiles we found do not say whether the community is gated.

An older part of Estero.

Sample listings in one real estate profile show homes built between 1988 and 1998, and the clubhouse was renovated in 2006. Sources do not give a single build window, so treat that as a clue and not a rule. If your home is from that era, it is about 28 to 38 years old.

That does not mean its pipes are bad. It means the parts that wear out with use, such as shutoff valves, toilet fittings, faucet cartridges and water heaters, have had a long run.

Attached villas and shared walls.

Villas and manor homes often share a wall or roof with a neighbor. A leak at one end of the building can show up in the middle. Tell dispatch whether you share walls, since the association may need to know about repairs that reach a shared line.

Governing documents decide who repairs what. Read them or call your manager before cutting into shared walls.

Copper Oaks, just east.

A 2013 planning study places Copper Oaks east of the Villages of Country Creek on Three Oaks Parkway, with 292 homes. It lists 129 single-family homes and 163 multi-family units, and says townhomes were built in 2005. It describes the community as gated.

We treat Copper Oaks as nearby, not as part of Country Creek, and we do not state its pipe materials either.

Sources: Estero Community Market Assessment (2013), Naples Condo Boutique profile of Country Creek.

When water shows up where it should not.

  1. 1Stop the flow.Turn the valve under the fixture. If that does not stop it, shut the main.
  2. 2Keep clear of live power.Outlets, cords and the panel are not safe to touch around standing water.
  3. 3Read the meter.With every tap off, a dial that moves means water is leaking somewhere.
  4. 4Phone us.Say where the water is and whether a neighbor shares the wall.
  5. 5Approve the plan.We tell you what failed and the price before the work begins.

If one line keeps failing, patching gets tiring. Our repiping page explains when replacing a run makes more sense than another repair. A burst pipe repair stops the emergency first.

Utilities and permits.

Your water bill shows which utility serves you. The Village divides service at Williams Road, and we do not assume which side your home is on.

The Village's Community Development department can say whether your job needs a permit. A water heater swap or repipe should not be assumed exempt.
