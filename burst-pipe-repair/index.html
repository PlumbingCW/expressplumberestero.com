Why pipes burst in Florida.

Freezing is not the main cause here, though a rare hard freeze can split exposed pipe. More often the culprits are corrosion, high water pressure, a bad fitting or a pipe that was nicked during other work. A sudden split can also follow a pressure surge after the water comes back on.

Many Estero homes are two to three decades old, so aging supply lines are a real factor. We cannot tell the cause from the phone, and the failed section often shows it when we cut it out.

What a burst looks like.

  • A hiss or spray behind a wall, in the attic or under a sink.
  • A ceiling that bulges, stains or drips.
  • Water appearing on floors with no fixture running.
  • A sudden loss of pressure throughout the house.
  • Wet drywall that softens or swells at the base.

How we repair a burst pipe.

Once the water is off, we find the failed section, cut it out and replace it with new pipe and proper fittings. We pressure test the line before closing anything, then restore your supply. Patches and clamps are used only as a stopgap.

Utility, permits and storms.

Water arrives from Lee County Utilities north of Williams Road and Bonita Springs Utilities south of it, per a Village of Estero water workshop document. Your main valve sits on the house side of the meter, and your utility bill tells you which utility serves you.

After storms like Hurricane Ian in 2022, supply restoration can bring pressure surges that stress old pipe. If water returns and something splits, shut the main and call. Ask the Village of Estero Community Development department whether your repair needs a permit.
