What we know and what we do not.

We know plumbing: water heaters, drains, sewer lines, leaks, fixtures and pipe. We know the Estero area well enough to say that many homes here date from the late 1990s through the mid 2000s, and that two water utilities split service at Williams Road according to a Village of Estero water workshop document.

We do not know your pipe type, your leak location or your drain problem from a phone call. Anyone who claims to is guessing. A plumber on site, sometimes with a camera or listening gear, settles it.

Honest limits.

We will not diagnose over the phone, quote a final price without seeing the problem, or tell you a permit is not needed. For permits, ask the Village of Estero Community Development department, or Lee County for unincorporated areas.

How to verify any plumber.

Florida plumbing contractors hold a state Certified Plumbing Contractor licence through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation. You can check any plumber, including us, at myfloridalicense.com. Use Verify a Licensee and search by name, licence number or county.

Confirm the status reads current and active. Ask the plumber for the licence number before work begins. A plumber who hesitates to give it is a plumber to pass on.

Before you hire any plumber.

  • Ask for the licence number and check it yourself.
  • Ask whether the company is licensed and insured.
  • Ask for the price in plain terms before work starts.
  • Ask who handles the permit, if one is needed.
  • Be wary of anyone who wants full payment up front for a repair.

Standards we follow.

Work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing. The 8th Edition took effect on December 31, 2023, and permits applied for after that date follow it. We explain what applies to your job in plain words.

On safety, we always tell customers the same few things. Shut off the water at the main if it is spreading. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility from outside. Never mix drain chemicals. Keep clear of electrical panels near water.

What we work on most.

Callers often start with a symptom, not a service name. A bill that keeps climbing, a wet wall, a heater with no hot water, a drain that clears and returns. Our pages are organized around those problems. Start with leak detection, drain cleaning or water heater repair if one of those sounds right.

Landlords and property managers also call for tenants. Tell dispatch who can approve the work and who gives access, and the visit goes faster.

Using this site.

This website has no forms and no online booking. Plumbing problems change by the minute, so the phone is the fastest route. Guides and service pages are general information and do not replace an on-site look. Details on the contact page show how to reach us.
