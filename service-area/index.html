Who supplies your water.

Williams Road splits two utilities.

According to a Village of Estero water workshop document from 2018, Williams Road is the dividing line in Estero. Bonita Springs Utilities serves south of Williams Road, and Lee County Utilities serves north of it.

Boundaries can change, so check your water bill for the provider name. It matters when you ask about water quality, hardness or a main break, because only your utility can answer for its own system.

Source: Village of Estero water workshop document, 2018.

What each utility's water means for your plumbing.

Bonita Springs Utilities blends shallow Lower Tamiami Aquifer wells, lime softened, with deeper Lower Hawthorn water treated by reverse osmosis. Lee County Utilities runs five plants into one combined system, using groundwater, reverse osmosis, nanofiltration and river water.

Neither source tells us your hardness. We could not confirm a figure, so ask your utility. If scale is building on fixtures or in a heater, our softener and filtration page explains your options. Sewer service varies by address, so ask your utility there too.

Permits depend on where you live.

Who to ask about a permit.

  • Estero homes: the Village of Estero Community Development department.
  • Bonita Springs homes: the City of Bonita Springs building department.
  • San Carlos Park and other unincorporated areas: Lee County.
  • Not sure of your jurisdiction: your property tax bill or the county can tell you.
  • Always ask whether your specific job needs one, since rules vary.

Permits applied for after December 31, 2023 follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, 8th Edition. A licensed plumber can explain which parts apply. You can verify any plumber at myfloridalicense.com.

Estero neighborhoods we get called from.

Estero has about 35 gated communities, including The Brooks, Shadow Wood, Grandezza, Pelican Sound, West Bay Club and Wildcat Run. Many homes there date from roughly the late 1990s to the mid 2000s, so water heaters and fixtures are aging. Gate access matters, so tell dispatch the gate procedure when you call.

Shopping and event areas such as Coconut Point, Miromar Outlets and Hertz Arena draw commercial calls too. Our commercial plumbing service covers those.
