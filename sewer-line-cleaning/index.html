Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Estero, FL

When the whole house drains slowly or sewage rises in the lowest tub, the main line is blocked. Call and we will clear it.

Main line clog or single drain clog.

A kitchen sink that drains slowly is a branch problem. A tub that fills when you run the washing machine is a main line problem. The main sewer line collects every branch, so a blockage there affects the whole house.

The lowest fixture in the house shows trouble first, because sewage rises where it has the least resistance. That is usually a ground-floor shower, a tub or a floor drain.

Signs the main line is blocked.

  • Several drains slow down at once, not just one.
  • A toilet bubbles or gurgles when another fixture drains.
  • Water backs into a tub or shower when you flush.
  • A sewage smell near floor drains or the cleanout outside.
  • Drains clear briefly after a plunger, then slow again.

What clogs a sewer line.

Roots are the usual cause outside the house. They enter through small cracks or loose joints and grow into a mat that catches paper. Inside, grease poured down kitchen drains cools and hardens in the main line. Wipes sold as flushable, feminine products and paper towels also build up where pipe bends or sags.

Mineral scale and old pipe wear make the inside of the line rough, and a rough wall catches debris faster. A line that clogs every few months is telling you something, and a camera view will show whether cleaning is enough. See sewer camera inspection for that step.

How we clear the main line.

  1. 1Find the cleanout.Most houses have a capped access pipe outside. If you cannot find it, tell dispatch and we will look.
  2. 2Run a cable machine.A motorized cable with a cutting head breaks through the blockage and cuts roots back to the pipe wall.
  3. 3Flush and test.We run water through the line and check that every drain flows freely.
  4. 4Camera check when needed.If the clog was heavy or came back before, we look inside to see the cause.
  5. 5Talk through prevention.We tell you what is likely to recur and what to keep out of the drains.

For heavy grease or scale, a cable alone may leave a film on the pipe wall. In that case hydro jetting scours the pipe with high-pressure water and leaves it cleaner. We recommend it when the line condition calls for it, not on every visit.

Do not mix chemicals, and other limits on DIY.

  • Never mix drain cleaning products. The fumes can be dangerous and the reaction can splash.
  • Chemical cleaners rarely reach a main line blockage and chemicals left standing in the pipe can splash a plumber's skin or eyes.
  • A hardware store drain snake can reach a sink trap but usually not a main line with roots.
  • Once sewage is coming up, stop using water so the backup does not grow.
  • Wear gloves and wash up well after touching any sewage.

Safety steps while sewage is backing up.

  • Stop running water, flushing and the dishwasher or washer until the line is clear.
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet area and wear gloves and boots if you must enter.
  • Never mix drain chemicals in a backed-up line, because the fumes and splashes are dangerous.
  • Wash hands well after any contact and discard soaked porous items such as rugs.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer before cleanup, then call for help.
