What West Bay Club is.

West Bay Club is a gated golf community in Estero with an 18-hole course. A real estate profile counts 688 homes, originally built in 1999, in about nine neighborhoods. A 2013 planning study lists ten names, including Chapel Ridge, Indigo Shores, Jasmine Bay North and South, Laurel Oaks, Riverbrooke, Sunset Trace and Turtle Point.

Homes are a mix of single-family houses, villas and condos. A local development summary also lists 26 new villas at West Lake Court, described as under construction when it was written. So you may find original-era homes and brand-new ones within a few streets.

Counts and neighborhood lists differ by source and change as new phases open. We use about and roughly where the sources disagree, and we do not publish exact boundaries.

Tank or tankless for a villa.

Villas and condos often have a closet sized for a tank heater. Swapping to a tankless unit may need a new gas or power supply, venting and a different water line setup. That is a job to plan, not a snap decision.

If your heater is old and the closet is tight, we can walk through both options. See tankless water heaters and water heater installation for what each involves.

After a storm, check before you assume.

Hurricane Ian made landfall on Sept 28, 2022, and Hurricane Irma passed through the area in 2017. Southwest Florida homeowners know to check plumbing after a storm. We do not claim any specific damage in West Bay Club.

  1. 1Look for wet spots.Check ceilings, closets and the floor around water heaters and toilets.
  2. 2Check the meter.With every tap off, watch the dial. Movement means water is going somewhere.
  3. 3Test hot water.If the heater was off during an outage, make sure it relights safely and holds temperature.
  4. 4Run each drain.Slow drains after a storm can point to debris in the line.
  5. 5Call about what you find.Describe the problem and send photos if you can.

Estero River and the bay.

West Bay Club is described as sitting along the Estero River as it nears Estero Bay. We make no flood or water level claim about any lot here. Your insurer or the Village can tell you about your property.

What we can say is that wet yards after rain are not always leaks. A meter that stops moving when every tap is off points away from a supply leak.
