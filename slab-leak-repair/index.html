What a slab leak is.

Most Florida homes sit on a concrete slab, and water lines often run through or beneath it. When one of those lines pinholes or splits, water escapes into the soil and concrete. You do not see it, but you can often feel, hear or measure it.

Hot water lines give the clearest signals because they warm the floor above. Cold lines leak quietly, and the first clue may be a bill that climbs for no reason.

Signs of a slab leak.

  • A warm or hot spot on a tile or wood floor.
  • The water meter keeps moving with every fixture off.
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on.
  • Damp carpet, lifting flooring or cracked grout along a line.
  • Low water pressure, or a water bill that rises with no change in use.
  • Wet soil or a damp patch along the outside edge of the slab.

What to do the moment you suspect one.

  1. 1Check the meter.Turn every fixture off and watch the leak indicator at the meter. If it still moves, water is going somewhere.
  2. 2Shut the main valve.Close the main water valve to the house. If the meter then stops, the leak is on the house side.
  3. 3Turn off the water heater supply.If the leak is on a hot line, switch off the heater so it does not run dry.
  4. 4Look for electrical hazards.If water is near outlets or a panel, stay clear and call. Do not stand in water around electricity.
  5. 5Call the 24/7 line.Report what you saw and heard. Dispatch will schedule the right equipment.

A meter spinning with everything off is a leak until proven otherwise. Call now.

Finding the leak without tearing up the floor.

We never start by opening the slab. Electronic listening gear picks up the sound of escaping water, and thermal imaging shows warm lines under the floor. Pressure testing isolates which line is losing water. Together these narrow the location to a small area before anything is cut.

No tool is perfect, and a deep or very small leak can take more than one method. We tell you how sure we are before recommending a repair. For the detection side by itself, see leak detection.
