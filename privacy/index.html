Privacy Policy for Estero Express Plumbing

This site has no forms and no accounts. Here is what we collect, what third parties load, and how to ask questions.

Call (239) 522-0887

The short version.

Estero Express Plumbing runs a call-based website. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts and no online checkout. If you want help, you call. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the site or phone our dispatch line.

We write this in plain language and keep it modest. It describes how the site works today. It is not legal advice, and it does not promise more than a small static website can deliver.

Information you give us by phone.

When you call (239) 522-0887, dispatch may ask for your name, the service address, a callback number and a description of the problem. We use that to schedule a visit, send a plumber and follow up on the job.

Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality and training. You will hear a notice if recording applies, and you may end the call or tell us you do not agree. We do not sell call details.

What dispatch may ask you.

  • Your name and a callback number.
  • The address of the property where work is needed.
  • A description of the problem and when it started.
  • Whether you rent or own, and who can give access.
  • Details such as heater brand, age or fixture type.

What the website itself collects.

This site is a set of static pages. It does not ask you to log in, and it does not contain contact forms. Our hosting provider and network infrastructure may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address, browser type and pages requested. These logs help keep the site secure and working.

Third-party content on our pages.

Two kinds of outside content load when you open pages. Fonts are served from Google Fonts. Maps showing our address and service area are embedded from Google Maps. When your browser requests them, those providers can receive technical details such as your IP address. Their handling is covered by their own privacy policies.

How to limit third-party loading.

You can block embedded content or fonts with your browser settings or an extension. The site remains readable, and the phone number stays available at the top of each page.

Phone links and texts.

Tapping the phone number on a mobile device opens your phone app and places a call. Your carrier handles that call under its own terms. If you send a text or email to the contact address, we use it only to reply. Please do not send urgent emergencies by email, because the phone is faster.

How we use and share information.

  • To schedule visits, dispatch plumbers and provide the service you asked for.
  • To confirm prices, follow up on work and answer questions.
  • To meet legal, tax, insurance or licensing duties when they apply.
  • With people who help us complete your job, such as a plumber assigned to your visit.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Keeping and correcting information.

We keep job records for as long as business, legal and accounting needs require. If you want a record corrected or removed, contact us and tell us what you want changed. Some records may need to stay in place for legal reasons. Rules differ depending on where you live, and we will tell you what we can do.

Children and changes to this page.

This site is meant for adults who own, rent or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children. We may update this page as the site changes, and the newest version stays on this URL. Questions? Visit our contact page or call the office. Our service details are on the services page.

Questions about privacy? Call and a person will answer.
