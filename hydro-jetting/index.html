What hydro jetting is.

Hydro jetting pushes high-pressure water through a hose and nozzle inside a drain line. The nozzle sprays water forward and back, which scours the pipe wall. Where a cable machine pokes a hole through a clog, jetting washes the buildup off the walls.

That makes it a good fit for lines coated with grease, soap scum, scale or fine silt. It is not the answer to every blockage, and we say so when it is not.

When jetting makes sense.

Good candidates for jetting.

  • A kitchen or main line that clogs again within weeks of being cabled.
  • Heavy grease buildup in restaurants, kitchens or multi-unit buildings.
  • Slow drains caused by scale or soap residue across a long run.
  • Light root growth after a camera look confirms the pipe is sound.
  • Preventive cleaning for a line with a history of backups.

When we hold off.

Jetting is not safe in every pipe. A cracked, collapsed or badly deteriorated line can be damaged by pressure. We run a camera first when the condition is unknown, and we do not jet a pipe that cannot take it.

If a camera shows damage, see sewer line repair instead.
